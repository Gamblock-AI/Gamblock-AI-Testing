# PKM-KC Structured Usability and SUS Protocol

## Status and boundary

This is a **planned** protocol for the next participant study. It is not a
record of collected data and does not change the completed formative feedback
activity with nine UTY students into a SUS study. That earlier activity found
an uninstall-permission issue and informed the emergency flow; its identities,
raw feedback, consent material, recordings, and observations are deliberately
not in this repository.

Before recruitment, the team must confirm the appropriate campus/authority
requirements for this activity, including participant information, voluntary
consent, withdrawal handling, safeguarding, and any required ethics or
academic review. Product settings consent is not research consent.

## Objective and scope

Measure prototype task usability and perceived usability after a controlled,
non-browsing task session. This study is formative. It must not claim reduced
gambling behavior, clinical effect, safety efficacy, or protection accuracy.

Use a dedicated Research/test build and only controlled simulated detection
fixtures. Do not ask participants to visit gambling services or enter personal
browsing data.

## Tasks

The facilitator gives neutral written task prompts and records only the
pre-approved aggregate outcomes below.

1. Find the protection state and activate/deactivate the permitted test
   protection flow using the normal consent surface.
2. Complete a controlled simulated protection trigger, observe the Pattern
   Interrupt, and reach the recovery/psychoeducation destination.
3. Find the recovery action and return to the primary protection view.
4. Follow the test-only accountability/uninstall request route, including the
   documented emergency alternative when the normal approval path cannot be
   completed.

The facilitator records task completion, assistance needed, and optional
participant comments outside the repository. A participant can skip a task or
withdraw without a penalty. Stop the session if a participant appears
distressed, requests support, or if the fixture exposes real browsing content.

## SUS instrument and analysis

After the tasks, administer the authorized Indonesian SUS wording associated
with Sharfina and Santoso (2016), while retaining the instrument source and
approval materials outside this public repository. Do not reconstruct a
historical SUS score for the earlier nine-person formative activity.

Score each completed SUS response using the standard 0--100 conversion: for
positive-numbered items subtract 1; for negative-numbered items subtract the
response from 5; add the ten adjusted values and multiply by 2.5. Report the
participant count, completed/withdrawn count, mean, median, standard
deviation, and a clearly labeled confidence interval or uncertainty method if
the approved analysis plan includes one. Interpret the score as perceived
usability in this prototype session only.

## Data and publication contract

Keep participant identity/contact data, raw answers, task notes, recordings,
screenshots, consent forms, device serials, timestamps, URLs, domains, DOM
content, browser history, and logs outside version control in the approved
institutional location. Do not use the public evidence ledger for these raw
materials.

If institutional approval permits a public project summary, it may contain
only: protocol version, study status, aggregate participant/completion counts,
aggregate task completion and assistance counts, aggregate SUS statistics, and
approved limitations. Publish no result until its governance status and
aggregate disclosure are confirmed. The canonical project testing report
remains unchanged unless a future approved evidence contract explicitly adds a
safe human-study aggregate section.
